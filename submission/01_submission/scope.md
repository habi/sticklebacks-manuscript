---
title: Scope for "High-Throughput Micro-CT and Automated Segmentation for Eco-Evolutionary Studies: A Case Study from Threespine Stickleback"
author: David Haberthür
date: 2026-08-24
---

We describe an efficient, reproducible method for extracting individual specimens (here, stickleback) from large multi-specimen micro-CT datasets and preparing them for automated morphological analysis.
The method is readily adaptable to diverse datasets and addresses a growing challenge in ecology and evolutionary biology: converting large datasets into reproducible, individually analyzable data.
