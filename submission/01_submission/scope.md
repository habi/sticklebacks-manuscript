---
title: Scope for "High-Throughput Micro-CT and Automated Segmentation for Eco-Evolutionary Studies: A Case Study from Threespine Stickleback"
author: David Haberthür
date: 2026-08-24
---

Our manuscript describes an efficient method to reproducibly and efficiently extract individual specimen (here: Stickleback) from large multi-specimen micro-CT datasets and preparing them for in-depth automated morphological analysis of the single specimen.
The method is applicable (and easily adaptable) to many different many-species datasets and addresses a growing methodological challenge in ecology and evolutionary biology: efficiently converting large tomographic datasets into reproducible, individually analyzable data.
