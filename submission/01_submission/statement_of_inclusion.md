title: "Statement of inclusion for *High-Throughput Micro-CT and Automated Segmentation for Eco-Evolutionary Studies: A Case Study from Threespine Stickleback*"
author: David Haberthür
date: 2026-08-25
---

Our study brings together authors from a number of different countries, including scientists based in the country where the study was carried out.
All authors from IEEE were engaged early on with the research and study design to ensure that the diverse sets of perspectives they represent was considered from the onset.
Lake sampling was carried out in collaboration with and included outreach with members of the Salamatof Native Association (SNAI) and Alaska Department of Fish and Game (ADFG). 
Where applicable, the lakes in our sampling are referred to with their local and original names.
